This flow uses coverage search, not ML or number prediction. In a fair draw, selecting different Classic numbers does not increase expected profit. Group optimization redistributes coverage and outcomes. Gold Ball is excluded.

How coverage is measured

Each ticket contains 15 pairs and 20 triplets. Repeated coverage is the total occurrences minus the distinct subsets across the group. The seeded search compares 64 candidate groups with an initial random group by default. These counts are separate from win rates and dollar returns.
